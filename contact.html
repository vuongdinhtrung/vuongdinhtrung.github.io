Collaboration

I welcome collaboration with partners in academia and industry on smart materials, functional polymers and fibers, wearable sensors and electronics, AI for materials and sensing, and energy harvesting. On the engineering side, I am also open to projects in mechanical design, CNC machining and 3D printing, from prototypes to small-batch production.

Students interested in smart materials, 3D printing or wearable electronics are welcome to get in touch.
