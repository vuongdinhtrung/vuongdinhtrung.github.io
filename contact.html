Collaboration

I am looking for partners in academia and industry on smart fibers, textile and skin-inspired wearable sensors, AI-assisted signal recognition for smart textiles, and droplet microfluidics / hydrovoltaic energy harvesting. Prototyping help with CNC machining and 3D printing is also welcome.

Students interested in fiber sensors, 3D printing or wearable electronics are welcome to get in touch.

Equipment sourcing & precision machining

Together with EYH Industrial, a precision engineering company in Ho Chi Minh City where I serve as Technical Advisor, I can help research groups and companies import and procure laboratory and manufacturing equipment, and arrange precision CNC machining of custom parts, jigs and fixtures, from one-off prototypes to small production runs. Send me your requirements or drawings and I will put you in touch with the right team.
